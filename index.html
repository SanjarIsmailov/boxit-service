Plan better. Move easier.

Simple tips that can save time, reduce costs and help moving day run smoothly.

01

Reserve parking early

Check parking restrictions at both addresses and arrange permits before moving day where needed.

02

Send a walk-through video

03

Label every box

Write the room and contents on two sides of each box so unloading and unpacking are much faster.

04

Measure large furniture

Measure doors, staircases and lifts before moving sofas, wardrobes or oversized items.

Keep essentials separate

FAQ

Useful answers before booking.

Do you help with loading and unloading?

Yes. We can provide a driver only or a driver with one or more helpers, depending on the job.

Can you dismantle and reassemble furniture?

Yes, this can be added to your booking. Please mention the items when requesting your quote.

Do you work outside London?

Yes. BOXIT is London-based and can complete long-distance jobs across the United Kingdom.

Can I send photos or a video?

Yes. Photos and walk-through videos help us understand the volume and provide a more accurate quote.

What about parking, tolls and charges?

Parking arrangements, tolls, congestion charges and similar costs are discussed before the booking where applicable.

What is your cancellation policy?

Bookings cancelled at least 24 hours in advance can be cancelled free of charge, subject to the agreed booking terms.
